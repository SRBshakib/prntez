How it works

Three simple steps to get your files printed.

1. Scan QR Code

Scan the shop's QR code with your phone's camera to open their upload page.

2. Select Files

Choose your documents and set your print preferences (color, copies, sides).

3. Get Job Number

Receive a 4-digit code. Show it at the counter and they'll print it instantly.
